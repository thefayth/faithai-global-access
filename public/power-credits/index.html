POWER CREDITS

Usable resources for builders with less access.

A program framework by Faith Cheltenham and the Cheltenham Institute for AI & Human Reciprocity.

Program state: preparation. Reading this page or creating a local request card does not submit an application, guarantee an award, or reserve donated resources. Live allocation needs verified contributions and human reviewers.

What a Power Credit can provide

Donated software access, API credits, compute, devices, connectivity, training, or technical assistance. Power Credits are noncash program resources. They are not a currency, investment, tradeable token, or promise of monetary return.

Who the program is for

People and small organizations facing practical resource barriers. The program has a reparative emphasis on Black communities and welcomes low-resource builders across identities, including women, caregivers, disabled people, rural communities, and poor White people. Polished applications or donor relationships must not substitute for need and usable benefit.

What contributors must disclose

  • Exactly what is usable, where, and for how long.
  • Restrictions, expiration, activation requirements, and any payment-card requirement.
  • Who receives data, what support is available, and what happens to unused resources.
  • Whether independent recipients can retain ownership of what they build.

Access must not depend on testimonials, photographs, sensitive identity disclosure, or public gratitude.

How reviewed distribution should work

  1. A person prepares a short request.
  2. A human reviewer checks available resources and restrictions.
  3. The person reviews an offer and chooses whether to accept.
  4. Delivery and activation receive separate receipts.
  5. An accessible appeal and correction path remains available.

Donated tools and tokens remain separate from operating income and restricted financial contributions. No donor gains control of allocations by contributing more.

Prepare one next step

Open FAITHAI, choose More Help, then Power Credits to prepare a local tool-gap card. Save or export only what you choose. The current local card does not send information to a donor.
