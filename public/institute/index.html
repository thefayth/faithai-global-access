Who receives the power created by AI?

Founded and authored by Faith Cheltenham. The Institute's digital work examines access, ownership, accountability, and the practical resources people need to build.

Founding framework: July 2026. Digital release: November 10, 2026. Governance proposals below remain open for review.

Our purpose

Study who receives AI's benefits, who bears its costs, and how technology can return usable capacity to the people and communities that help create it. Our work begins with Black communities and remains open to builders facing resource barriers across identities and places.

Working charter

  • Preserve people's authorship, ownership, and ability to leave or export.
  • Make core guidance available without payment, an account, an advertisement, or proof of identity.
  • Publish sources and distinguish research, proposals, prototypes, and verified live services.
  • Keep affected communities close to decisions, with understandable complaints and appeals.
  • Measure resources activated and used, rather than announced retail value.

Research and program index

The current research framework includes the Suresha–Cheltenham Model, Cultural Physics, Token Shaming, Imploitation, Power Credits, and AI's social and environmental accounting. Manuscripts and research claims require their own source review before publication here.

The Suresha–Cheltenham review asks about representation, decision power, usable resources, trust, community proximity, barriers to access, donor concentration, and exclusions hidden by overall averages.
