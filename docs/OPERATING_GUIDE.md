# FAITHAI beta — operating guide

## Start, return, and leave

1. Choose **Start one thing**, then one numbered focus. A useful action card is ready within a few choices.
2. Choose **Save** only if you want the card retained on this device. **Export** downloads text; it does not send it to another person.
3. **Continue my journey** or `H` reopens saved steps. Move steps between NOW, NEXT, LATER, and DONE without scores or streaks.
4. **Find help** or `M` opens books, farm/food, aid, private safety guidance, challenges, and program information.
5. **Learn or play** opens two English-language offline activities. They offer a useful saved practice action, not rankings.
6. `C` opens Classic; returning preserves this session. Explicit shared-device clearing removes FAITHAI's named saved keys and resets the session.
7. `Q` switches to neutral weather notes. It cannot erase browser/network history. Safety exploration is session-only and must not be exported automatically.

Typing, number keys, short letters, and touch work without a microphone. Speak requires separate disclosure and consent; a browser speech service may process audio externally. FAITHAI does not store audio. Do not use speech on a device or in a place where it would expose you.

## Current service boundaries

- Free local guidance is the launch mode. OpenAI is configured server-side but **off by Faith's choice** after exhausted API credits. No automatic retries, top-ups, or billing changes.
- Emergency support is guidance only, not monitored crisis chat, dispatch, or live response.
- Bundled information and synthetic signals are labeled prototypes. Production reviewed/signed local-service packs are not yet published. Check authoritative local sources before high-impact decisions.
- Invitations/challenges are local demonstrations; Power Credits are noncash program preparation. They are not live applications or guaranteed allocations.
- SMS/USSD is a simulator, not a connected carrier.
- No third-party ads or real payment collection are active.
- BiNet's help lane is English in this first beta; other language work still needs community review. Learning games are English-only.

## Restore and share carefully

Recovery creates an encrypted file and a separate alphanumeric code. Keep them apart. Anyone holding both can open the saved state. Recovery does not create an account or cloud backup. The current file-text recovery interface still needs better feature-phone parity and file-upload usability.

The five bundled language interfaces and Arabic RTL can be explored locally. Their presence does not mean every book, game, provider, or territory has been human-reviewed or translated.
